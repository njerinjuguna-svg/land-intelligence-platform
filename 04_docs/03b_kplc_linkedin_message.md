## IF HE ASKS WHAT YOU WOULD DO WITH IT

Answer in distances, not in layers. It is the difference between sounding
like someone who wants a copy of their database and someone who wants to
answer a question.

- "Nearest medium voltage line: 340 m" on a plot page, sourced to Kenya
  Power, with the date of the extract.
- A flag on any plot that intersects a wayleave corridor, saying what the
  restriction is.
- Nothing about whether a specific plot can be connected, what it would cost,
  or how long it would take. That is Kenya Power's answer to give, and the
  buyer gets pointed to them rather than a guess.

That last point is worth volunteering. It is what separates a data partner
from a liability, and it costs nothing to say.

## WHAT NOT TO PROMISE

No equity, no fee, no exclusivity in a first message. Do not say the data
will be made public. Do not pitch it as good for Kenya Power's brand.
