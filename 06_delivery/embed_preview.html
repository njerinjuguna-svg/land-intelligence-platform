We do not recommend this plot for holding as an investment — it scores 32 out of 100 for that. The reasons are below.

Can I build a house here?
Not on all of it

Part of this plot falls inside a river reserve, which by law you cannot build on.

Have a surveyor mark the reserve on the ground before you plan anything. What is left may still be buildable.
Can I farm it?
Yes — this is good growing land

Heavy, reliable rain of about 1,564 mm a year.

Proper farming land. If it also floods, that is the same water that makes the soil rich — plan for it rather than against it.
Will it flood?
Yes, it floods

A large part of this plot is in the highest flood categories, and so is the land around it.

Expect water here in a heavy season. Ask neighbours how high it came last time — they will know better than any map.
Is this area growing?
Yes, steadily

There is real growth in night-time lights, but not at the pace of the fastest peri-urban areas.

Steady area. Buy for what you will use it for, not for a quick rise.
Before you pay
  1. Ask the county where the river reserve runs
  2. Have a builder price the foundation for black cotton
  3. Ask neighbours how high the water came last season
  4. Confirm the boundary with a licensed surveyor
  5. Have your advocate do an official search at the Lands office
Everything here is measured from satellite images, terrain, rainfall records and public datasets. Nobody from our team has stood on this land. This is not a valuation and not a legal opinion. It does not confirm who owns the land or where its legal boundary runs. Flood risk is worked out from the shape of the land, not from flood records. It cannot see a broken dike or a blocked drain. Always visit the plot and use an advocate and a licensed surveyor before you pay any money.
